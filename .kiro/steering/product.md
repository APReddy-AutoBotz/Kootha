# Product Steering - Kootha / Prachar

Kootha / Prachar helps local businesses verify that mic advertisement vehicles completed promised work.

The product should feel trustworthy, simple, local-business friendly, low-cost, practical for small towns, and usable by non-technical drivers and customers.

Main promise:

> You pay for local announcement. We give you proof that it was really done.

## User Experience Rules

- Customers do not install an app in v1.
- Customers receive simple updates and final proof reports.
- Admin controls what is shared with customers.
- Drivers use simple screens with large buttons.
- Use plain English and prepare for Telugu labels later.
- Do not overbuild premium features before the core proof flow works.

## Business Rules

- Customer live tracking is premium only.
- Driver consent is required if customer live tracking is enabled.
- Phone location is the default low-cost tracking method.
- Vehicle GPS device is optional and integration-ready only until later milestones.
- Tracking stops after work is completed.
- Manual proof photos are important.
- Manual audio/video proof can be added when needed.
- Do not secretly record audio.
