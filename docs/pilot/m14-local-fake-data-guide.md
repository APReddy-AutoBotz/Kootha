## Fake Ad Work

- Title: Demo Market Announcement Dry Run.
- Package: Standard.
- Planned days: one or two test days.
- Areas: Ongole Main Road, Kurnool Road Junction, Addanki Bus Stand Area.
- Proof needed: photo proof and Phone Location Proof for test only.
- Customer update plan: manual copy/share only.

## Fake Proof Notes

- Announcement completed near demo market area.
- Demo proof photo uploaded for test-only review.
- Customer update copied manually.

## Do Not Use

- real customer names,
- real driver names,
- real business owner mobile numbers,
- real Work Codes,
- real proof file paths,
- real GPS coordinate traces,
- real Supabase URLs or keys,
- screenshots containing real private data.

## Local-Only Rule

If a fake record is created in a shared Supabase project, label it clearly as dry-run data and delete it only after AP confirms it is no longer needed for review. Do not mix fake dry-run records with a real customer pilot.
