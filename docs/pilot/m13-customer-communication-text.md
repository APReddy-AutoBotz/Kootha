# M13 Customer Communication Text

Use this wording when explaining the pilot to a customer. Keep the message simple and do not add unsupported proof promises.

## Before Work

Your Prachar mic advertisement work will be planned by date, area, and package. Our team will assign an approved driver and vehicle before release.

## During Work

We may share manual updates by phone call, manual WhatsApp, manual SMS, in person, or another manual method. These updates are handled by our team; they are not automatic provider messages.

## Proof After Work

After work, we will review photo proof and prepare a Final Proof Summary. If Phone Location Proof was required, our admin team may include a customer-safe summary after review.

Safe wording examples:

- Phone Location Proof: Reviewed by admin
- Phone Location Proof: Needs follow-up
- Phone Location Proof: Not required
- Phone Location Proof: Not available

## Important Limits

Phone Location Proof is supporting evidence only. It is not a live tracking link, not a public map, not certified route proof, and not a distance billing record. The customer summary will not include exact coordinate values, internal review notes, tracking session records, or private proof file paths.

## Final Sharing

The Final Proof Summary may be copied, printed, or manually shared by the admin team. It is not sent through an automatic WhatsApp/SMS provider in this pilot.
